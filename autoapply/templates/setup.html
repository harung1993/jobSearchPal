{{ "✓" if s.gmail.done else "6" }} Gmail{{ s.gmail.note }}

Lets JobSearchPal read job-alert emails (LinkedIn, Indeed and others), sort replies from companies, remind you to follow up, and email you a morning brief. It reads only, never marks anything read, and never sends anything except the brief to you.

An app password is not your normal password. Turn on 2-step verification, then create one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. It's saved in data/secrets.json on this server, readable only by you, and never shown again.
