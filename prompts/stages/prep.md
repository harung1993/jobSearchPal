Interview prep packs. Follow AGENTS.md. `autoapply list --prep` lists applications with an interview email and
no pack yet (or "update": true for a new stage). For each, write the pack in Markdown to its `prep_path` (create
the folder). Keep research proportionate: about 5 searches per pack.

Use the interview emails to tell the stage (recruiter screen, hiring manager, technical, panel) and focus on it:
- **The stage**: who you're meeting, format and length if the email says, what this stage usually tests.
- **What candidates report**: the process and questions others have reported, from public pages only (Glassdoor
  or Indeed interview pages readable without signing in, Reddit, Blind, levels.fyi, the company's blog). Link
  every source. If little is public, say so; never make questions up and call them reported.
- **Likely questions for this role**: from the posting's requirements (label these "from the posting").
- **Your stories**: 5-6 behavioral questions, each with a short STAR answer from real experience in materials/
  and CLAUDE.local.md, with real numbers.
- **About them**: what they do, recent news with links and dates, the team.
- **Questions to ask** (5-8) and **Pay** (the ask from notes.md, and the posted range).

Print one line: "Prep packs: <Company> (<stage>), ..." or "No prep packs needed".
